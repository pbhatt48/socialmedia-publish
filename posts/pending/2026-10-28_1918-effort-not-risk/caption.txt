"The rate of return sought should be dependent, rather, on the amount of intelligent effort the investor is willing and able to bring to bear on his task." — Benjamin Graham, The Intelligent Investor

Graham was rejecting a popular idea: that the return you aim for should rise with the risk you are prepared to take.
In his framework, the defensive investor accepts a modest return in exchange for safety and freedom from concern.
The enterprising investor may aim higher, but only by supplying more analysis, judgment and diligence, not more risk.
Ambition without the work behind it is just speculation with a target.

Which kind of investor are you, honestly, given the hours you actually put in?

Learn. Think. Allocate. Compound.
