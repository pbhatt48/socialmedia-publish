"The great lesson in microeconomics is to discriminate between when technology is going to help you and when it’s going to kill you." — Charlie Munger, 1994 USC Business School Talk

Munger’s example was Berkshire’s old textile mill. A better loom cut costs, but in a commodity business the savings flowed straight through to the customers, not to the owner.
Every competitor could buy the same machine, so the gains were competed away.
Technology helps an owner only when there is a moat to keep what it saves.
Without one, a new machine can just mean spending more money to stay in a poor business.

Which business today is spending heavily on new technology that will mostly benefit its customers?
