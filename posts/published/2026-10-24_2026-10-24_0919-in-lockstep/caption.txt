"Charlie and I cannot promise you results. But we can guarantee that your financial fortunes will move in lockstep with ours for whatever period of time you elect to be our partner." — Warren Buffett, An Owner’s Manual, Berkshire Hathaway

Notice what is refused here: a forecast. What is offered instead is a structure.
Returns are uncertain for everyone; alignment is a choice that management either makes or avoids.
When the people allocating capital own a large stake on the same terms as you, their mistakes cost them too.
Before asking how much a manager might earn you, ask how much of their own wealth sits beside yours.

How much weight do you give insider ownership when you evaluate a company or a fund?
