"The supreme irony of business management is that it is far easier for an inadequate CEO to keep his job than it is for an inadequate subordinate." — Warren Buffett, 1988 Letter to Berkshire Shareholders

A subordinate answers to a boss whose own results are being measured. The CEO often answers to no one in that sense.
Buffett's explanation is structural: performance standards for the top job are rarely explicit, and board relations tend to be cordial.
For owners, this is why the board and the incentives matter as much as the manager. Weak leadership can persist for years.
