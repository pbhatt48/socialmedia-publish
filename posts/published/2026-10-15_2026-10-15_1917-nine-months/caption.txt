"You can’t produce a baby in one month by getting nine women pregnant." — Warren Buffett, 1985 Letter to Berkshire Shareholders

Some outcomes are bounded by time, not by talent, capital or effort.
A brand earns trust, a culture takes root and retained earnings compound on their own schedule, and no amount of urgency shortens it.
Many of the costliest mistakes in business and investing come from trying to buy speed: overpaying for growth, overleveraging, abandoning a sound thesis because it has not paid off yet.
The investor’s edge is often just a willingness to let the calendar do its work.

What in your own portfolio or career are you trying to rush that simply needs more time?

Learn. Think. Allocate. Compound.
