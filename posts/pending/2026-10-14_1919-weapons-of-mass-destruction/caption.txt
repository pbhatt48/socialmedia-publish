"Derivatives are financial weapons of mass destruction, carrying dangers that, while now latent, are potentially lethal." — Warren Buffett, 2002 Letter to Berkshire Shareholders

Buffett wrote this while unwinding the derivatives book Berkshire inherited with General Re, years before 2008 proved the point.
The danger he described was not volatility but opacity: long-dated promises, valued by models, owed by counterparties tied to one another.
Risk that does not show up in the reported numbers has not disappeared. It is merely waiting.
An investor who cannot see a balance sheet's obligations clearly should assume they are larger than they look.

Where do you think today's latent risks are hiding?
