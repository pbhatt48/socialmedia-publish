"Rule No. 1: Most things will prove to be cyclical. Rule No. 2: Some of the greatest opportunities for gain and loss come when other people forget Rule No. 1." — Howard Marks, The Most Important Thing

Marks does not claim to know when a cycle will turn, only that it will.
Booms tempt people to extrapolate good times forever; busts tempt them to extrapolate pain forever.
Both mistakes are the same mistake: treating today’s conditions as permanent.
The investor who remembers the pendulum can stay cautious when others are euphoric and stay willing when others despair.

Where in today’s markets do you think people are most tempted to forget Rule No. 1?
