"Patient opportunism, waiting for bargains, is often your best strategy." — Howard Marks, The Most Important Thing

Most of the time, markets offer fair prices, not great ones. The discipline is in recognizing which is which.
Waiting is not the same as doing nothing. It means keeping a watchlist, knowing values in advance, and keeping capital and nerve ready.
The rare moments when price falls well below value usually arrive when selling is forced and sentiment is at its worst.
An investor who must always be fully active will pay whatever the market asks. One who can wait gets to name the price.

What do you do to stay prepared while you wait for a real bargain?

Learn. Think. Allocate. Compound.
