"Opportunity cost is a huge filter in life." — Charlie Munger, Berkshire Hathaway Annual Meeting

Munger judged every idea against the best one already available to him.
If a new investment can't beat what you already own or could easily buy, it doesn't need more study.
That one comparison removes most of the noise before analysis even begins.
It also explains how a concentrated portfolio can be the disciplined one: the bar keeps rising.
The question is never "Is this good?" but "Is this better than my best alternative?"
