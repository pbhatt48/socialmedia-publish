"It’s not supposed to be easy. Anyone who finds it easy is stupid." — Charlie Munger, as quoted in Howard Marks’s memo “It’s Not Easy” (2015)

Munger said this to Marks at the end of a lunch in 2011, while Marks was finishing The Most Important Thing.
Average results are easy to get: an index fund delivers them. Superior results require seeing something the crowd has missed, and the crowd is large, smart and well-funded.
If an approach feels effortless, the likeliest explanation is not that you have found an edge, but that you have not yet understood the problem.
Difficulty is not a sign that something is wrong. It is the price of admission.
