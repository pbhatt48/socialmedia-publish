"Keep things simple and don’t swing for the fences." — Warren Buffett, 2013 Letter to Berkshire Shareholders

Buffett wrote this for investors who are not experts: know your limits and choose a course that is certain to work reasonably well.
Complexity feels like rigor, but it mostly adds new ways to be wrong.
The long swing gets the attention. Compounding comes from the many hits nobody notices.
Avoiding the big error does more for your returns than reaching for the big win.

Where in your process are you still swinging for the fences?

Learn. Think. Allocate. Compound.
