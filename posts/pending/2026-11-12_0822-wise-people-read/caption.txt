"In my whole life, I have known no wise people (over a broad subject matter area) who didn’t read all the time — none, zero." — Charlie Munger, Poor Charlie’s Almanack

Munger's point is narrower and harder than it sounds: breadth of judgment requires breadth of input.
Reading lets you borrow other people's decades, their mistakes, and their models, at almost no cost.
For an investor, it is the raw material of a circle of competence and of the patterns that recur across industries and cycles.
Knowledge compounds like capital: each book makes the next one more useful.
