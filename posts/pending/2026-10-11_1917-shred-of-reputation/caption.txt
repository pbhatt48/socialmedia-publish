"Lose money for the firm, and I will be understanding; lose a shred of reputation for the firm, and I will be ruthless." — Warren Buffett, Testimony to Congress on Salomon, September 4, 1991

Buffett said this as interim chairman of Salomon, a firm whose standing had been badly damaged by a Treasury bond bidding scandal.
The asymmetry is the lesson. A financial loss is bounded and can be earned back; lost trust compounds against you for years.
His test for employees was simple: would you be comfortable seeing the act on the front page of your local paper, read by your family?
For an investor, management's integrity is not a soft factor. It is the one input every other number depends on.
