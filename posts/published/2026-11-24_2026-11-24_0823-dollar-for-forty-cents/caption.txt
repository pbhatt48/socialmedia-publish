"It’s extraordinary to me that the idea of buying dollar bills for 40 cents takes immediately with people or it doesn’t take at all. It’s like an inoculation." — Warren Buffett, The Superinvestors of Graham-and-Doddsville (1984)

Buffett’s observation is less about intelligence than temperament.
The arithmetic of paying 40 cents for a dollar is trivial; acting on it when the crowd disagrees is not.
In the same essay he notes that showing records for years rarely converts the unconvinced.
Value investing is a way of seeing before it is a set of techniques.

Did the idea “take” for you immediately, or did it arrive gradually?

Learn. Think. Allocate. Compound.
