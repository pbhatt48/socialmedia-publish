"The market, like the Lord, helps those who help themselves. But, unlike the Lord, the market does not forgive those who know not what they do." — Warren Buffett, 1982 Letter to Berkshire Shareholders

Buffett followed this line with a warning: paying too much for even an excellent company can undo a decade of favorable business progress.
The market rewards work done before the purchase: reading the filings, understanding the economics, knowing what a business is worth.
It does not grade on intent. A good company bought blindly can still be a bad investment.
Preparation is the bridge; price discipline keeps you on it.

What is the one piece of homework you never skip before you buy?

Learn. Think. Allocate. Compound.
