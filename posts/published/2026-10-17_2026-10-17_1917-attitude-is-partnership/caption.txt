"Although our form is corporate, our attitude is partnership." — Warren Buffett, Berkshire Hathaway Owner’s Manual

It is the first of the owner-related principles Buffett set down for Berkshire shareholders in 1983.
He went on to describe shareholders as owner-partners and himself and Charlie Munger as managing partners, and the company as a conduit through which owners hold its assets.
The framing works in both directions. Managers who think like partners treat retained earnings as someone else's capital. Shareholders who think like partners judge the business, not the quote.
A share is not a ticker. It is a fractional claim on a real enterprise, with a partner's rights and a partner's patience.

If you had to sit across the table from the management of your largest holding, would they treat you as a partner?

Learn. Think. Allocate. Compound.
