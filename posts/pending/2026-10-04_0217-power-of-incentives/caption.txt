"I think I’ve been in the top 5% of my age cohort all my life in understanding the power of incentives, and all my life I’ve underestimated it." — Charlie Munger, 1995 Talk, The Psychology of Human Misjudgment

Munger listed incentives first among the causes of human misjudgment, and still admitted he kept underrating them.
Before trusting a forecast, a pitch or a management plan, ask who gets paid, how, and for what.
Compensation tied to revenue, share count or deal volume quietly shapes capital allocation.
Read the proxy statement with the same care as the income statement.

What incentive structure has most changed how you judge a company?
