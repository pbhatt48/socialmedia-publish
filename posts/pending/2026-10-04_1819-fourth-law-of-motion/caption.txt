"For investors as a whole, returns decrease as motion increases." — Warren Buffett, 2005 Letter to Berkshire Shareholders

Buffett offered this as a "Fourth Law of Motion," the one Newton missed after losing heavily in the South Sea Bubble.
The arithmetic is simple: collectively, investors own the market and earn what its businesses earn.
Every trade, fee and switch is a cost that comes out of that shared return before anyone is paid.
Activity can feel like diligence. In aggregate, it is friction.

Which of your recent portfolio moves would have been better left undone?
