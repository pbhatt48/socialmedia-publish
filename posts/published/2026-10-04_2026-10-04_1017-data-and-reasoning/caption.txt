"You are neither right nor wrong because the crowd disagrees with you. You are right because your data and reasoning are right." — Benjamin Graham, The Intelligent Investor

Graham placed this among the closing principles of his book, right after urging investors to have the courage of their knowledge and experience.
Popularity is not evidence. A rising price doesn't validate a thesis, and a falling one doesn't refute it.
The discipline cuts both ways: being contrarian proves nothing either. Only the facts and the logic count.
So the real work is upstream: checking your data, testing your reasoning, and knowing what would change your mind.

What fact, if it turned out to be true, would make you sell your largest holding?

Learn. Think. Allocate. Compound.
