"Generally speaking, envy, resentment, revenge and self-pity are disastrous modes of thought." — Charlie Munger, 2007 USC Law School Commencement Address

Munger treated temperament as a working tool, not a personality trait.
Envy makes you chase what someone else owns; resentment and revenge keep you anchored to a past decision; self-pity turns a bad outcome into a reason to stop thinking.
Each one swaps the question "what is this worth?" for "how do I feel about it?"
Avoiding them costs nothing, and because they are the default human response, simply staying clear of them is an edge over most people.

Which of the four has cost you the most in your investing?
