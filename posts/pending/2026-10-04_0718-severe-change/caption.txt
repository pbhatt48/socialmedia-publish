"Severe change and exceptional returns usually don’t mix." — Warren Buffett, 1987 Letter to Berkshire Shareholders

Buffett observed that the best business returns usually come from companies doing much the same thing they did five or ten years earlier.
A business facing constant upheaval also faces constant chances for major error.
Ground that shifts violently is hard ground on which to build a fortress-like franchise.
Yet markets often pay the highest multiples for the promise of feverish change.

Which company you admire is doing almost exactly what it did a decade ago, and still earning more every year?
