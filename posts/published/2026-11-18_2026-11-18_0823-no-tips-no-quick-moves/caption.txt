“Don’t buy on tips or for a quick move. Let the professionals do that, if they can.” — Walter Schloss, Factors Needed to Make Money in the Stock Market (1994)

Schloss compounded capital for nearly five decades with a one-room office, no computer models, and a habit of reading balance sheets instead of listening to rumors.
A tip hands you a conclusion without the reasoning behind it, so you cannot tell when it has stopped being true.
A quick move is a bet on other people’s next decision, not on what a business is worth.
The quiet barb is in the last three words: the professionals rarely do it well either.

What was the last investment idea you acted on, and did it come from your own work or someone else’s?

Learn. Think. Allocate. Compound.
