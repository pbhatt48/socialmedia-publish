"The safest way to try to get what you want is to try to deserve what you want." — Charlie Munger, 2007 USC Law School Commencement Address

Munger called this the golden rule, so to speak: deliver to the world what you would buy if you were on the other end.
It holds for businesses as much as for people. Durable returns tend to follow durable value delivered to customers.
Companies that extract more than they give eventually invite competition, regulation, or a loss of trust.
For investors, the question is not only "how much will this earn?" but "does it deserve to?"

Which company you follow most clearly deserves its success, and why?
