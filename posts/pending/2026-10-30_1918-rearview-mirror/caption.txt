"In the business world, unfortunately, the rear-view mirror is always clearer than the windshield." — Warren Buffett, 1991 Letter to Berkshire Shareholders

Buffett wrote this about newspapers and TV stations, businesses that for decades looked like near-perfect franchises.
The past record was clean and easy to read. The change in the industry's economics was not.
Historical returns tell you what a business has been. Only an understanding of its economics tells you what it may become.
Valuation is an exercise in judging the road ahead, which is why a margin of safety matters.

Which business today looks safest in the rear-view mirror but faces the most uncertain road ahead?
