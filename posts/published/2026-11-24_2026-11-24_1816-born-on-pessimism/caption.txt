"Bull markets are born on pessimism, grow on skepticism, mature on optimism and die on euphoria." — Sir John Templeton, The Templeton Maxims

Templeton's line describes a market's mood, not its price.
At the bottom, the news is bad, prices are low and almost nobody wants to buy.
At the top, the news is good, prices are high and almost nobody worries.
The investor's job is to notice which stage the crowd is in, and to act against it with discipline.
That is why "the time of maximum pessimism is the best time to buy."
