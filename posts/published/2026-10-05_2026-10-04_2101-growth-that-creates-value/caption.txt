"Growth benefits investors only when the business in point can invest at incremental returns that are enticing — in other words, only when each dollar used to finance the growth creates over a dollar of long-term market value." — Warren Buffett, 1992 Letter to Berkshire Shareholders

Growth is not a virtue in itself. It is a use of capital, and capital has a cost.
A business earning less than its cost of capital destroys more value the faster it grows.
The real question is not how fast a company grows, but what each retained dollar earns.
That is why "growth" and "value" are not opposites: growth is simply one input into value.

Which company do you know that grows quickly but turns each new dollar into less than a dollar of value?
