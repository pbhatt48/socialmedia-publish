"The worst sort of business is one that grows rapidly, requires significant capital to engender the growth, and then earns little or no money. Think airlines." — Warren Buffett, 2007 Letter to Berkshire Shareholders

Growth is not a virtue by itself. What matters is what each new dollar of capital earns.
A business that must keep reinvesting just to stay in place turns shareholders into lenders who never get repaid.
In the same letter, Buffett wrote that investors had poured money into a bottomless pit, "attracted by growth when they should have been repelled by it."
Before admiring a company's growth, ask what it cost and who paid for it.

Which industry today do you think grows fastest while returning the least to its owners?
