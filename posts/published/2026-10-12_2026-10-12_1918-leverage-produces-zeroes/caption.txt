"History tells us that leverage all too often produces zeroes, even when it is employed by very smart people." — Warren Buffett, 2010 Letter to Berkshire Shareholders

Compounding is multiplication, and in multiplication a single zero erases everything that came before it.
Leverage magnifies good years, but it also gives creditors the power to force a sale at the worst possible moment.
Intelligence does not protect against this. A brilliant model with borrowed money is still one bad year from ruin.
The first rule of compounding is to stay in the game long enough to let it work.

How much of your long-term plan depends on never having a bad year?

Learn. Think. Allocate. Compound.
