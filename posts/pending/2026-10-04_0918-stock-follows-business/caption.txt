"Over the long term, it’s hard for a stock to earn a much better return than the business which underlies it earns." — Charlie Munger, 1994 Talk at USC Business School

Munger’s point: a business earning 6% on capital for 40 years will return roughly 6% to a long-term holder, even if bought at a huge discount.
One earning 18% for decades can reward you handsomely even at an expensive-looking price.
Over short stretches, multiples dominate returns. Over long ones, return on capital does.
The purchase price matters most when the holding period is short; the business matters most when it is long.
