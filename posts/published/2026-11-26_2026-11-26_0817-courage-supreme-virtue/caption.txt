"In the world of securities, courage becomes the supreme virtue after adequate knowledge and a tested judgment are at hand." — Benjamin Graham, The Intelligent Investor, Chapter 20

Graham puts the virtues in order. Courage comes last, and it only counts once the first two are in place.
Courage without knowledge is just recklessness, and knowledge without courage never turns into returns.
The hardest moment to act is usually when the work is finished, the price is right, and nobody agrees with you.
Investors who do the homework still miss their chance when they hesitate at that point.

Which do you find harder: doing the analysis, or acting on it when the crowd disagrees?

Learn. Think. Allocate. Compound.
