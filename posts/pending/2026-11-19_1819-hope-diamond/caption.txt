"It’s better to have a partial interest in the Hope Diamond than to own all of a rhinestone." — Warren Buffett, 2013 Letter to Berkshire Shareholders

Control feels like an advantage, but what compounds is the quality of the underlying business.
Owning 100% of a mediocre company means owning 100% of its problems and its low returns on capital.
A small stake in an exceptional business lets its economics, and its managers, do the work for you.
Public markets offer this every day: fractional ownership of companies you could never buy outright.

Would you rather own a small slice of a great business or all of an ordinary one, and why?

Learn. Think. Allocate. Compound.
