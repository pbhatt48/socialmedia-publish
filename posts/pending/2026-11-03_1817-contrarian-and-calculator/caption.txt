"Value investing is at its core the marriage of a contrarian streak and a calculator." — Seth Klarman, Margin of Safety (1991)

Each half fails on its own.
Contrarianism without arithmetic is just a mood: the crowd is often right, and buying what everyone hates is not the same as buying cheaply.
Arithmetic without a contrarian streak finds nothing: bargains rarely sit where consensus is comfortable.
The edge lives where both overlap — a willingness to stand apart, and the numbers to show the gap between price and value is real.

Which comes less naturally to you: going against the crowd, or doing the math when you do?

Learn. Think. Allocate. Compound.
