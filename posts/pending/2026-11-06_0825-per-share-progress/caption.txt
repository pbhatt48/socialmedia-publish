"We do not measure the economic significance or performance of Berkshire by its size; we measure by per-share progress." — Warren Buffett, Berkshire Hathaway Owner’s Manual

Size is easy to manufacture. Issue enough shares, borrow enough money, buy enough companies, and the empire grows.
The owner’s question is different: is my slice worth more than it was?
A business can double its revenue and leave each shareholder poorer if the growth was paid for with dilution.
Per-share intrinsic value is the scoreboard that keeps management honest.

When you read an annual report, which number do you check first: total growth, or growth per share?
