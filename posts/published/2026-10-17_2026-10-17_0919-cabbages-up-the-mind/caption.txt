"Another thing I think should be avoided is extremely intense ideology because it cabbages up one’s mind." — Charlie Munger, 2007 USC Law School Commencement Address

In the same speech, Munger compared intense ideology to a whirlpool that swallows even skilled canoeists who drift too close.
Investors have their own ideologies: "growth only," "never tech," "cash is trash," "the Fed decides everything." Each feels like conviction and works like a blindfold.
His remedy was strict: don't hold an opinion until you can argue the other side better than its own advocates can.
A thesis you can't argue against is one you don't fully understand yet.
