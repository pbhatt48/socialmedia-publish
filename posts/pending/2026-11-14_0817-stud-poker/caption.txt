"Investing without research is like playing stud poker and never looking at the cards." — Peter Lynch, One Up on Wall Street (1989)

A share price is public. The business behind it has to be studied.
Reading the filings, understanding how the company earns its money, and knowing what could break it is the work of looking at your cards.
Skip it, and you are not taking a position. You are only placing a bet.
Research won't remove uncertainty, but it tells you when the odds are actually in your favor.
