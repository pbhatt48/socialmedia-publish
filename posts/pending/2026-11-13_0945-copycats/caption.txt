"Invest in the copycats rather than the innovators." — Mohnish Pabrai, The Dhandho Investor (2007)

Pabrai's example is the Patel family motels: no new idea, just a proven model run frugally, then repeated.
Innovators carry the cost of every experiment and every failure. Copycats inherit the evidence.
Picking which new idea wins is genuinely hard; recognising a business model that already works, and a management that executes it well, is far more tractable.
Originality is admirable in a founder. In an investment, it is often just another source of uncertainty.

Which great business do you admire most for copying an idea and executing it better?
