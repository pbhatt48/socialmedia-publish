"The person that turns over the most rocks wins the game." — Peter Lynch, 1997 PBS Frontline Interview

Great investments rarely announce themselves. They sit under rocks most people never bother to lift.
Lynch’s edge at Magellan was less a secret formula than a volume of honest work: more companies visited, more reports read, more questions asked.
Most of what you examine will be ordinary. That is not wasted effort; it is the cost of recognizing the exception when it appears.
Breadth of search and discipline of selection are two halves of the same craft.

How many companies do you study for every one you actually buy?
