"A truly great business must have an enduring “moat” that protects excellent returns on invested capital." — Warren Buffett, 2007 Letter to Berkshire Shareholders

High returns on capital are an invitation. Competitors will come.
What matters is not how wide the moat is today, but whether it is widening or narrowing.
Low cost, a trusted brand, switching costs, network effects: each is a barrier, and each must be maintained.
A moat that needs a brilliant manager to defend it is not much of a moat.

Which company do you think has the most durable moat today, and what would it take to breach it?
