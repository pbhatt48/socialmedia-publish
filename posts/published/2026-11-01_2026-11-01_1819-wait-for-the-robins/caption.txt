"So if you wait for the robins, spring will be over." — Warren Buffett, "Buy American. I Am." (The New York Times, October 2008)

Buffett wrote this weeks into the financial crisis, admitting he had no idea where stocks would go over the next month or year.
His point was narrower than a forecast: markets tend to turn well before sentiment or the economy does.
By the time the good news is visible to everyone, it is usually already in the price.
Acting on value while the headlines are still bad is uncomfortable, which is precisely why it is rewarded.

What signal are you waiting for before you act, and would it come too late?
