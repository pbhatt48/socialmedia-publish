"Volatility is far from synonymous with risk. Popular formulas that equate the two terms lead students, investors and CEOs astray." — Warren Buffett, 2014 Letter to Berkshire Shareholders

Volatility measures how much a price moves. Risk is the chance of permanent loss, including the slow loss of purchasing power.
A diversified collection of good businesses will swing far more than cash, yet over decades cash has often been the riskier holding.
The investor who mistakes swings for danger may sell at the lows or hide in assets that quietly erode.
What matters is the horizon, the price paid, and whether you will ever be forced to sell.

How do you define risk in your own portfolio?
