"If your behavior and that of your managers is conventional, you’re likely to get conventional results – either good or bad." — Howard Marks, “I Beg to Differ,” Oaktree Memo (2022)

Doing what everyone else does all but guarantees you get roughly what everyone else gets.
To beat the average, your positions have to differ from the consensus.
But being different is only half of it. Unconventional and wrong is just a quicker way to lose money.
What earns outperformance is differing from the crowd and being right, and being willing to look wrong while you wait.

Where in your portfolio are you truly differing from the consensus, and how sure are you that you're right?
