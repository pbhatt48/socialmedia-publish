"The best stock to buy may be the one you already own." — Peter Lynch, Beating the Street (1993)

It is one of Lynch's own "Peter's Principles," and it cuts against the instinct to always be hunting for something new.
A company you have owned and followed for years is one you understand better than almost anything on a screener.
If the story is intact and the price is attractive, adding to it can carry less risk than starting over with a stranger.
The discipline is to re-examine each holding as if you were buying it fresh today, not to fall in love with it.

When did you last re-underwrite a position you already hold, and did it make you add, hold, or sell?
