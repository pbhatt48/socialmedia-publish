"Don’t ask the barber whether you need a haircut." — Warren Buffett, 2009 Letter to Berkshire Shareholders

Buffett wrote this about acquisition advice: bankers paid on completed deals rarely argue against the deal.
The lesson travels well beyond Wall Street. Advice is shaped by how the adviser gets paid.
Before you weigh a recommendation, ask who benefits if you say yes, and who benefits if you do nothing.
Good advice often costs the adviser something. Be suspicious of advice that always ends in action.

Whose incentives do you check first before acting on a recommendation?

Learn. Think. Allocate. Compound.
